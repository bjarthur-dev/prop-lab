## Lessons
1. **Close capture missed 10 of 11 NFL games and every NBA game.** CLV, the only fast signal (Rules §8), exists for 5 of 35 entries. Check that the 75-minute look-ahead fix (commit 667e4f3) catches the 1:00 window next Sunday.
2. **NFL WATCH rows went 16–12 but lost 4.0% CLV on all five that have a close.** Results flatter the model. Judge it on closes.
3. **N1 is about zero-catch mass.** Every N1 under that won big was a 0, and every loss was a back or TE who drew 3+ targets. Logging targets or route share at freeze time would let us test that.
4. **Low-volume WR overs die on injuries and one-catch games** (Shaheed hurt with 1 yd, Mitchell 0 on 3 targets, Meyers 0). The model's 0.58–0.60 on these looks too high.
5. **StatMuse game pages are the most reliable box source the morning after.** ESPN/CBS/FOX/Yahoo pages are often frozen pre-game or mid-game even when they say "Final".
