## Lessons
1. **Price-edge CLV was +6.8% and +4.9%, while the model's WATCH legs were −4.2% and −3.7%.** It's only 2 entries per segment, but it is the split the rules predict. Keep logging price_edge rows even when they're not placed.
2. **Discounted Pick6 lines are negative EV by construction.** A 68% break-even against a ~48% estimate cost $5 (17% of the roll) in one entry. The plan already says standard picks only, and this is the reason why.
3. **API-NBA drops low-minute players and new rookies** (Lendeborg, Hopkins, Thomas, Stirtz, Ament). The team-sum check catches it, so fill from StatMuse/Covers. ESPN boxes are often frozen mid-game.
4. **One-game clusters inflate tracker arms.** All three H3 wins were from CHI@BOS.
