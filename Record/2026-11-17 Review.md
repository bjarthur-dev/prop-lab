## Lessons
- **The day was profitable only because of the promo.** The 30% SGPx paid +$22.17. The two Pick6 Gimme entries each died on a ~52% "fair" leg (Quickley, Towns), and with them the Gimme promo's value. A 3-pick at 6x still needs two near-coin-flip legs to hit (about 27%), so losing both was the likeliest result.
- **The card's favorite unders moved against us across the board (−6.5% CLV on 4)**, and went 1–3. Favorite-under CLV was also negative on 11-12 (−6.4%), 11-14 (−3.9%) and 11-16 (−3.2%). The source is losing to the close, not just on results.
- **The H3 blowout theory needs the blowout to happen.** SAS (−10) lost outright, and the hyp arm went 3/8. The spread trigger doesn't control for upset risk.
- **Reading CBS box pages:** the box is past character 100k, so read it with `offset=100000`. The CBS DET@SA page needed permission, so the fallback is Fadeaway World or Covers.
