## Lessons
- **The system was up and the live in-game bets sank the day.** The system went +$20.87 and the live bets −$31.80. The user's call (Lessons 11-12) is no blanket ban, but every live bet gets priced and logged with a reason. That night's $23 bet was 72% of the bankroll at the time, on a high-hold coin flip.
- **Favorite unders, CLV −6.4%** on all three watches, with the market moving away from us before tip. They're 4 days negative in a row. Treat "favorite under at −150 to −170" as a boost-only leg type.
- **H3 has flipped negative (−6.8 on 34).** Spread ≥ 10 doesn't predict reduced minutes when the game goes to OT, and one 2OT game wipes out a night. It's still noise (SE 10.3), but there's no sign of an edge.
- **ESPN live box snapshots can settle a bet** when the stat is already past the line (James 5 ast, Goodwin 2 3PM at Q3). Counts only go up, so a mid-game number past the line is a valid second source for that result.
