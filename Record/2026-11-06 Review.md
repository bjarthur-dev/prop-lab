## Lessons
- Rebound unders on the favorite in a likely blowout aren't safe: the bench and role players still got 19–29 minutes, and blowout minutes can go to them.
- "Most points" props on a star in a blowout lose to minutes: SGA played 30 minutes while Mitchell got hot.
- Covers matchup pages (`/sport/basketball/nba/matchup/<id>`) and plaintextsports (`/nba/<date>/<away>-<home>`) had final full boxes when ESPN, CBS and nba.com didn't. Find the URLs by search first.
