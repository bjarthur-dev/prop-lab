## Lessons
1. **The under-lean "BETs" lost, but with positive CLV.** Mitchell and Harden both closed toward the under (+2.5% and +2.0%) and still lost. A two-bet sample says nothing. Keep tracking CLV on the gate-blocked sides: if they keep beating the close, the lean may be partly real; if not, it's the minutes bias.
2. **Minutes bias check:** the card had Wembanyama projected for 28 minutes. Rotowire (single source) says he played 29, in a 16-point win. D. Mitchell played 37 against a projected 29. Starters' minutes are still running above the engine's numbers.
3. **The MLB short leash again:** Rasmussen at 89 pitches and 11 outs. A K over on a postseason starter needs pitch-budget risk priced in. Both WS K entries had negative CLV.
4. **H1a triggers need a played/void rule.** Irving (b2b rest) was a predictable DNP risk. Check the official report for rest on the second night of back-to-backs before freezing the row.
5. **Pipeline:** GitHub's scheduled `results` cron ran ~3 h late on 10-24 and hadn't fired by 08:20 today. The morning task should dispatch it when the D file is missing (done by hand today).
