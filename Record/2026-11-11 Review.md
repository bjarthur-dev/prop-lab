## Lessons
- **The late update's "line moved, drop it" rule has cut the winning leg two nights running** (Tatum, Barrett). When the book moves *off* our line toward our side (2.5 → 1.5 for an under), that's the market agreeing with us. It could be re-priced at the alt line instead of dropped.
- **Favorite legs keep showing small negative CLV (−1.1% today, −3.9% on 11-10).** They're paid for by hit rate, not price. Only take them with a fresh boost.
- **H3 has gone flat (net +0.9 on 31).** The spread ≥ 10 games keep ending closer than priced.
- **Basketball-Reference and CBS had the late OKC@POR finals** that ESPN and Yahoo didn't. Use them first for West Coast games.
