## Lessons
- **SI's scoreboard pages have full named box scores with minutes in seconds,** and they were the fastest reliable second source for gap fills. ESPN's rows are unnamed (matched by roster order), and CBS pages are often live snapshots.
- **For NFL settlement, team-site box scores (newyorkjets.com, packers.com, Titans, Saints, Dolphins) were final when ESPN and CBS weren't.** But the Browns, Raiders and Rams sites were stale snapshots too, so check each page for a final score.
- **Unders beat the market on Sunday across the board:** NFL rec-yds unders (both N1 arms) went 33/53 and NBA starter pts unders 16/27. One night is noise, and the N1 control beating N1 is the real reading.
- **The system's own bets went 1–3, and the user's off-card live bets made the day.** The CAR/IND parlay staked the whole balance on two −280/−256 favorites. It won, but a loss would have zeroed the account.
