## Lessons
- **"Blowout spread" doesn't mean a blowout happens.** Both of 11-14's ≥ 12.5-point favorites failed to blow anyone out (CHI +15.5 won outright). H3 needs a lot of games before it means anything, and nights like this are why. Keep logging it; don't size off it.
- **Favorite unders logged at −EV prices lose CLV on schedule.** They went 3–2, but CLV was −3.9%. Same as 11-09 to 11-12: the hit rate is noise and the price is the signal.
- **Tracker arm labels need to be exactly `hyp` or `control`.** Anything else silently drops out of the grade.
- **Close timing:** the 19:11 close missed a 19:10 tip by one minute. A close at T−10 for each tip window would fix it.
