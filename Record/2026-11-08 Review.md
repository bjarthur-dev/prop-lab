## Lessons
1. **Don't add a filler leg to make an SGPx legal.** Both card legs won, and the user-added Murphy u2.5 3PM leg (no sharp check) killed a +535 ticket. If a slip needs a third same-game leg, it has to clear the same pass rule as the others, or the slip is skipped.
2. **Moonshot sizing broke every cap.** $68 of the $85 went into a no-edge casino game, including $40 = 67% of the roll. Under §9 this is the "bet placed that wasn't on the card" kill-switch condition again.
3. **Alt-rung paper slips need a close capture,** or they can never be graded on CLV. Add the NFL alt rungs to the pre-kick close pull.
4. **Shadow files must carry `date`.** One missing column silently blocked grading until it crashed.
