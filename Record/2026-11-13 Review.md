## Lessons
- **The boosted 3-leg unders are the profit line again.** 290 cashed and 289 is likely to, while the unboosted Bet & Earn step 1 lost on a player whose teammate (Murphy III) was ruled out. The late re-price at 18:35 had already flagged Fears steaming to the over.
- **Price-edge watches beat the stand-in close on all four,** at +7.3% mean, but went 1–3. That's the 104-leg pattern in small: CLV +3.1% with ROI still negative. It's still the slowest-converging segment to trust.
- **API-NBA can drop a whole late West Coast game**, not just low-minute players. The morning fill has to check the game count against the odds slate, not only `_gaps.csv`.
- **The close job runs once at 21:04 ET, after most tips.** On a 9-game night that leaves 7 games without a true close. It needs a run per tip window (≈ T−10 for 19:10, 20:10 and 21:40 or later).
