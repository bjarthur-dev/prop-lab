## Lessons
- A steam veto has to come before placement. When boosted SGPs are built at 14:53 and the 18:19 check shows sharp movement against a leg, it's already too late. Build promo SGPs after the pregame check, or only from legs that already have a steady 2-snapshot history.
- Big-man rebound unders in neutral-site games are fragile: Zubac had 12 rebounds in 19 minutes. A short-minutes projection doesn't protect a rebounding under.
- Pick6 legs taken off the market favorite went 1–2. Durant at 53.5% was the weakest leg, and it lost. Keep the ≥ 55% floor per leg.
