# Market Intel

Every slate teaches you something about *the market itself*. Log it here — this file is the compounding edge.

## The lessons so far

### 2026-10-22 (TNF + NBA)
- **Reverse line movement is the tell**: OKC opened -7.5, steamed to -8.5 while 75% of tickets were on Denver +8.5 — sharp money on the Thunder against the public. Check open→current + splits on every game before pricing props.
- **Edges get consumed fast**: SGA over 30.5 was +102 when we logged it, -111 an hour later. Our entry will grade with big positive CLV — but the lesson is that when you find +EV, the market may be moving with you. Log immediately, bet the moment you decide.
- **Cross-market disagreements are real**: Polymarket had Jokic 10+ assists at 54¢ while books implied ~46% on 9.5-over. One side is wrong. We played the book side at +108.
- **Line-shop the alternates, not just the books**: Gordon's rebound main was 5.5 at -140; the 6.5 alternate was +120. Same thesis, better structure.
- **Integer pick'em lines ≠ half-point lines**: PM's "6+ assists at 62¢" and the books' "6.5 over at 48%" are the SAME probability once you add the P(exactly 6) mass (~13-14 points). Don't confuse a line-construction artifact with a disagreement.
- **Pick'em apps vs sportsbook pricing**: fixed multipliers demand 54.7-57.7% per leg vs 52.4% at -110 — the same pick can be +EV at a book and -EV on an app. Check both every time.

## CLV reading rules
- Raw same-line price movement (not devigged) + fair-close EV (needs both closing sides) — two separate diagnostics, never approximated.
- Positive CLV on 55%+ of a large sample = you're taking better numbers than the market's final opinion. That's the skill, even when results lag.
