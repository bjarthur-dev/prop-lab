## Engine fixes made this run (`engine/nfl/build_shares.py`)
1. **OUT matching dropped the wrong player.** The name key turned both "Brian Robinson" and "Bi.Robinson" into `brobinson`, so listing Brian Robinson as OUT also deleted **Bijan Robinson** and handed his share to teammates. One "T.Johnson" entry likewise hit three players. Matching now requires the same last name, a full first name that starts with the pbp initials, and the same team (an optional `team` column in nfl_out.csv).
2. **Redistribution capped carry shares at 45%.** `clip(upper=0.45)` was applied to carry shares too, on every team with an OUT player, which cut RB1s (J. Taylor 0.65→0.45, Bijan, Marks, Knight). The result was fake 30-pt rush-yard unders (Bijan u90.5 showed mean 55). Now targets cap at 45% and carries at 75%, and an existing share is never cut. After the fix, Bijan rush yards have a mean of 92.5 against the 90.5 line.
3. **Still open, not fixed in the engine:** `run_nfl.py` keys its simulations by short name, so two players with the same short name on the slate collide. Malik Washington (MIA) was being priced as Mike Washington Jr. (LV RB): u41.5 at 98%. For this run I worked around it in the input builder by giving the 12 duplicate names a team suffix (`M.Washington|MIA`). The engine should key by (player, team).
